# History retains weekly goals and earned Personal records

History introduces Weekly goals from the active Plan's training frequency and Personal records from individual logged sets. Plan frequency and activation currently overwrite their previous values, and measurement-system changes convert and round historical weights. Recomputing everything from today's Plan and weights would therefore change what past achievements mean.

Each Monday–Sunday week retains its original Weekly goal; changes to Plan frequency take effect the following Monday. Existing weeks without a reliably known goal show Workout Session counts without a success or failure judgment. They are not assigned today's Plan frequency as an invented historical target.

The first tracked partial week can earn a goal hit and start a Weekly streak without a penalty for missing its goal. It is excluded from the completed-week hit-rate calculation. A completed week without a known goal is neutral but breaks the streak; it does not pause a streak across an unknown period.

An earned Personal record is retained across later measurement-system changes. Converting and rounding weights must neither remove an earned badge nor award one solely because the User changed units. First-ever results establish a baseline and ties earn no record.

Existing Workout Sessions receive Personal records by evaluating their available recorded results chronologically, then retaining the awards. This cannot recover original weights or units lost to earlier conversions; it uses the evidence that remains rather than inventing earlier results.

This trades additional historical state for stable achievements. Deriving past goals from the current Plan or recalculating earned badges after every unit change would be simpler, but would rewrite the User's training history. Monthly totals still use the recorded weights in the User's current measurement system; they are not frozen achievements.
